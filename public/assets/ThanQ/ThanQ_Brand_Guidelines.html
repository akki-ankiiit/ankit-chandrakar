A thank-you should reach the person who earned it.

ThanQ is an appreciation app. It lets anyone thank a waiter, a delivery partner, a stylist or a housekeeper in a few seconds. One scan, one tap, and the person behind the service knows they were seen.
ThanQ is simply how you spell thank you, shortened the way people say it.

Primary logo

Setting type

Sentence case.
Capital letters at the start of sentences and names only. All caps is for logos and acronyms.
Left aligned, ragged right.
Centre only short lines such as a thank-you message.
Lines under 64 characters.
Wider and the eye loses its place.
Ember is for big things.
Type in ember must be 24 px or larger. Smaller orange text uses burnt ember #B64D20.
Space in multiples of 8.
8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. It keeps layouts calm and quick to build.

Graphic
language

The heart is a window

Photography can appear inside the heart. It turns the symbol into a frame for a real moment between two people.
Once per layout. One heart, one photo.
Centre it on the exchange. Faces and hands, not backgrounds.
At least 240 px wide. Smaller and the faces vanish.
