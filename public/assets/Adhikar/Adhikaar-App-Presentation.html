Contractor loyalty app

Adhikaar

Points, portfolios
and rewards

A Kamdhenu Adhesive app that helps contractors track points, explore rewards, and present their work.

The brief

Contractor loyalty, with tools
for their work

01

Make rewards visible

Adhesive purchases earn points. Contractors need to see their balance and what those points can unlock.

02

Help present their work

Contractors need a practical way to turn site photos and project details into a portfolio they can share.

03

Keep it approachable

The requirements called for a simple interface, with company support for photography, portfolios, and tour coordination.
