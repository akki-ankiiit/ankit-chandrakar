Product design case study

Local proof for
field sales

An internal Tejas tool that helps relationship managers show where its products are already used.

The problem

A product claim is hard
to trust on its own

RMs explain product quality during sales visits. Customers and contractors still need a reason to believe it.

The missing piece is a relevant example: someone they know, or a project close to them.

The question Atlas helps answer

Who around me
has used this
product?

The insight

Familiar use
makes proof
more credible

What informed the direction

A review of sales documents and conversations with contractors.

01

A familiar place

A nearby project makes adoption easier to relate to.

02

A recognizable person

A neighbour, relative, friend, or known local figure adds relevance.

03

Work they can see

Project images and customer accounts support the conversation.

The solution

Put local references on a map
